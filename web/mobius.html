What the reversible odometer can—and cannot—undo

The tracker knows the initial phase constellation and the actions. The neural learners do not. Even with exact read composition, hidden noise is not undone by an inverse of known actions. These checks diagnose the geometry, not learned memory compression.

Interpretation and boundaries

The exact-law particle filter has the same observation access as the trained models, but gets the full known dynamics and a distribution over initial shapes. It is an informed classical observer, not parameter-matched. The full-state oracle has privileged information and is a separate lower reference.

What did not happen: the qubit-inspired learner did not beat the classical known-law filter. This three-seed CPU screening cannot establish general superiority over GRUs or transformers, and the transformer is deliberately tiny with an 8-token window.

What did happen: exact first-harmonic reads compose, shape invariants remain constant, and second harmonics or unseen drift break the simple reversible-state story. That makes this an out-of-family test for Q-word's previously planted quantum-memory task.
